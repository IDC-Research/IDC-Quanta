IDC Quanta puts IDC's market data and analyst research directly in your chat. Every figure comes from a live IDC source with a citation you can follow, so nothing here is answered from memory.

## What you can ask

- **Market share & competitive standing** — "who leads in [market]?", vendor share and rankings, share movement over time, and how a specific vendor is performing against its rivals (share gains and losses, win/loss trends).
- **Market sizing & forecasts** — "how big is the [market]?", TAM/SAM/SOM, CAGR, and growth by segment, vertical, or geography.
- **IT spending outlook & budgets** — the short- and long-term IT spending outlook and revisions, "how much is [industry] spending on [technology]?", and how your own IT budget allocation compares with peer benchmarks.
- **Technology sourcing (for a purchase you're making)** — resolve your need to an IDC market, set your requirements, get a scored vendor shortlist and a side-by-side comparison, then build an RFP or prepare for negotiation.
- **Vendor evaluation & MarketScape** — who the IDC MarketScape Leaders are in a market and how vendors are positioned, for a competitive or market view.
- **Competitive comparison** — how a named competitor is positioned against you on IDC's evaluation criteria, their analyst-documented strengths and cautions, and the market-share evidence behind each.
- **Competitor strategy & early signals** — a synthesized read on a competitor's strategy and intent ("what has [vendor] done, where are they headed?"), plus early detection of competitor and market moves (M&A, launches, pricing, partnerships).
- **Emerging technology & transformation** — whether and when to adopt an emerging technology, its maturity, business impact, and risk, plus technology-driven transformation opportunities by use case.
- **Executive, investment & M&A** — CFO, board, and investment-case market context, and an acquisition target's market position grounded in IDC share and growth.
- **Market landscape & opportunity** — the market and share dynamics behind a strategic choice, and sizing or validating an opportunity in a given market, vertical, or geography.
- **Research, quotes & answers** — "what does IDC say about [topic]?", a merged research-plus-data answer, and citation-ready verbatim analyst quotes.

Just ask in plain language — no command needed, though `/idc-quanta` also works. Some answers depend on figures you provide, and where IDC has no company-specific data you'll get the closest industry or cohort benchmark, labelled as one.
